# Downloaded character artwork

Source: Platformer Characters by Kenney
https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-characters
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (see kenney/License.txt).
Downloaded from the creator's website. The six adventurer PNG poses in kenney/
are unmodified copies from PNG/Adventurer/Poses/ in the original pack.
The downloaded source ZIP is retained as kenney-characters.zip.

The local SVG block, flag, spikes, and original player are teaching artwork
created for this demo. The background is drawn in scenery.js using Canvas.
