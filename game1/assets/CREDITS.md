# Downloaded character artwork

Source: Platformer Characters by Kenney
https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-characters
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (see kenney/License.txt).
Downloaded from the creator's website. The six adventurer PNG poses in kenney/
are unmodified copies from PNG/Adventurer/Poses/ in the original pack.
The downloaded source ZIP is retained as kenney-characters.zip.

The local SVG block, flag, spikes, and original player are teaching artwork
created for this demo. The background is drawn in scenery.js using Canvas.


## Animated enemies and goal flag

Source: New Platformer Pack v1.1 by Kenney
https://kenney.nl/assets/new-platformer-pack
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (see kenney-platformer/License.txt).

Unmodified PNGs copied from the creator's downloaded archive:
- Sprites/Enemies/Default/slime_normal_walk_a.png and slime_normal_walk_b.png
- Sprites/Enemies/Default/bee_a.png and bee_b.png
- Sprites/Tiles/Default/flag_yellow_a.png and flag_yellow_b.png

Stored in kenney-platformer/. The original archive is kenney-platformer.zip.
The original local enemy.svg and flag.svg are retained as simple teaching examples;
the running game now uses the downloaded PNG animation frames instead.

## Sword

sword.svg is original local artwork created for this demo: a transparent sword sprite with a steel blade, gold guard, and leather grip. No external asset requests are needed.
